# 6-Week Race Training Plan: 25-Mile Gravel Race

**Target Race Date:** Saturday, October 17, 2026 (~40 Days Out)  
**Distance:** 25.0 miles  
**Surface:** Gravel & mixed terrain (Bentonville, AR)  
**Primary Goal:** Transform 25 miles on gravel from an exhausting one-off effort into a repeatable, paced, and comfortable performance.

---

## 1. Athlete Context & Assessment

- **Current Longest Ride:** 30.3 miles (2026-09-06, 2h48 moving, 965 ft climbing, peak HR 183 bpm, Session Load 1,344).
- **Core Insight:** You have already proven you can cover the distance. The training task is *not* building up raw mileage from scratch—it is building aerobic efficiency, pacing discipline, sit-bone saddle endurance, and shedding fatigue so race day feels controlled.
- **Key Focus Areas:**
  1. **Absorb Sunday's Load Spike:** 1,344 session load is 2× the previous baseline maximum (671). The opening week must be light to avoid deep autonomic fatigue.
  2. **Sit-Bone / Saddle Issue Resolution:** Discomfort at 2h48 needs addressing in Week 1 with bib/chamois testing and saddle position adjustments.
  3. **LTHR Field Test Calibration:** Anchor true heart-rate zones using the 20-minute threshold test in Week 2 rather than relying on untested percentage-of-max formulas.
  4. **Benchmark Repeat (Week 3):** Re-test the 16-mile Greenway Benchmark under the exact baseline protocol to measure cardiovascular adaptation 4 weeks in.
  5. **Structured 2-Week Taper:** Drop training volume while maintaining short intensity openers to arrive on race day with peak freshness (positive TSB).

---

## 2. Pacing & Nutrition Strategy

- **Flat / Rolling Gravel:** Strict **Zone 2** (Endurance). Never burn matches on flat sections early in the race when other riders sprint.
- **Climbs & Punchy Kickers:** Cap effort at **Zone 4** (Threshold). Stand only when necessary for cadence relief; avoid sustained Zone 5 surges.
- **Hydration:** 1 bottle (500–750 ml) with electrolytes per 45–60 minutes.
- **Fueling:** 30–50 grams of carbohydrates per hour (chews, gel, or carb drink mix) starting at minute 30.
