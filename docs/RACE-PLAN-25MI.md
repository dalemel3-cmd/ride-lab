# 6-Week Race Training Plan: 25-Mile Gravel Race

**Target Race Date:** Saturday, October 17, 2026 (~40 Days Out)  
**Distance:** 25.0 miles  
**Surface:** Gravel & mixed terrain — the real course runs through the remote
Ozark highlands crossing into southern Missouri: canopy-covered gravel roads,
**rocky climbs**, a few paved stretches, and **water crossings**. This is not
the smooth crushed-limestone gravel around Bentonville — see the terrain note
under Phase 3.  
**Primary Goal:** Transform 25 miles on gravel from an exhausting one-off effort into a repeatable, paced, and comfortable performance.

---

## 0. Adjustments made after the first review (2026-09-07)

Three changes from the version Antigravity originally generated, plus a
clarification. Reasoning kept here rather than only in chat, so the "why"
survives past this session.

1. **Terrain mismatch.** Every gravel session below trains on smooth local
   surfaces (the Greenway, or repeats of the Brassy Pot loop). The real course
   is rocky and technical. Handling a loose, rocky descent at race pace while
   tired is a skill, not just fitness, and nothing in the original plan built
   it. **Fix:** one of the Saturday "Gravel Endurance" rides (Sep 19 or 26) is
   now a dedicated rough-surface / bike-handling session on the loosest,
   rockiest terrain reachable, not another smooth-gravel volume day.
2. **Tire advice conflict.** An earlier session recommended a faster-rolling
   semi-slick (Terra Speed) for the mostly-paved training mix. That is the
   wrong tire for *this race* — rocky terrain and water crossings want tread
   and puncture protection, not less of both. **Fix:** any tire upgrade is
   scoped to training rides only; race day runs the stock Terra Trail (or
   equivalent tread) unless a race-specific tire is chosen *and tested* well
   before race week. Nothing untested touches the bike on Oct 17.
3. **Crank-bolt checks were only on the pre-race checklist.** The left crank
   has already worked loose twice this study (Aug 27, Aug 29). Checking it
   once, the day before the race, is too late to fix anything at a shop.
   **Fix:** added a weekly torque check starting this week, not just Oct 15.
4. **Fueling rehearsal wasn't tied to a specific day.** "30–50g carbs/hour"
   is a target, not a rehearsed habit. **Fix:** the Oct 3 rehearsal and Oct 16
   shakeout both now specify riding with the *actual* race-day food, bottles,
   and carry setup — not just "something to eat."

- **Current Longest Ride:** 30.3 miles (2026-09-06, 2h48 moving, 965 ft climbing, peak HR 183 bpm, Session Load 1,344).
- **Core Insight:** You have already proven you can cover the distance. The training task is *not* building up raw mileage from scratch—it is building aerobic efficiency, pacing discipline, sit-bone saddle endurance, and shedding fatigue so race day feels controlled.
- **Key Focus Areas:**
  1. **Absorb Sunday's Load Spike:** 1,344 session load is 2× the previous baseline maximum (671). The opening week must be light to avoid deep autonomic fatigue.
  2. **Sit-Bone / Saddle Issue Resolution:** Discomfort at 2h48 needs addressing in Week 1 with bib/chamois testing and saddle position adjustments.
  3. **LTHR Field Test Calibration:** Anchor true heart-rate zones using the 20-minute threshold test in Week 2 rather than relying on untested percentage-of-max formulas.
  4. **Benchmark Repeat (Week 3):** Re-test the 16-mile Greenway Benchmark under the exact baseline protocol to measure cardiovascular adaptation 4 weeks in.
  5. **Structured 2-Week Taper:** Drop training volume while maintaining short intensity openers to arrive on race day with peak freshness (positive TSB).

## 2. Pacing & Nutrition Strategy

- **Flat / Rolling Gravel:** Strict **Zone 2** (Endurance). Never burn matches on flat sections early in the race when other riders sprint.
- **Climbs & Punchy Kickers:** Cap effort at **Zone 4** (Threshold). Stand only when necessary for cadence relief; avoid sustained Zone 5 surges.
- **Hydration:** 1 bottle (500–750 ml) with electrolytes per 45–60 minutes.
- **Fueling:** 30–50 grams of carbohydrates per hour (chews, gel, or carb drink mix) starting at minute 30.
